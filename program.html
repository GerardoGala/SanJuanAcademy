Program for Dinghy Sailing 101

An introduction to the fundamentals of sailing a small dinghy.

Dinghy Sailing 101 is designed for students who are beginning their journey into dinghy sailing.

The program combines online instruction with in-person sailing instruction.

1. Safety

  • Personal safety
  • Personal flotation device (PFD)
  • Weather and wind awareness
  • Basic emergency procedures

2. Boat Orientation

  • Parts of the dinghy
  • Hull
  • Mast and boom
  • Rudder
  • Centerboard or daggerboard
  • Understanding the boat before getting underway

3. Standing Rigging

  • Mast
  • Shrouds or stays, where applicable
  • Forestay, where applicable
  • Checking the rig
  • Rigging the boat correctly

4. Controls

  • Tiller
  • Mainsheet
  • Centerboard or daggerboard
  • Understanding what each control does
  • Coordinating the controls while sailing

5. Launching

  • Preparing the boat for launch
  • Checking the rig and controls
  • Launching at beam reach
  • Getting underway

6. Steering & Points of Sail

  • Steering relative to the wind
  • Close-hauled
  • Close reach
  • Beam reach
  • Broad reach
  • Running

7. Sail Trim

  • Trimming the sail
  • Letting the sail out
  • Finding the correct sail position
  • Adjusting the sail as the boat changes direction

8. Tacking

  • Preparing for the tack
  • Turning through the wind
  • Changing sides
  • Trimming the sail after the tack
  • Recovering from an unsuccessful tack

9. Gybing

  • Preparing for the gybe
  • Boom awareness
  • Safe body position
  • Controlled sail movement
  • Trimming after the gybe

10. Landing

  • Planning the approach
  • Reducing speed
  • Approaching the landing area
  • Coming alongside safely

11. Retrieving the Boat

  • Securing the boat
  • De-rigging
  • Removing the boat from the water
  • Proper storage

12. Capsize Recovery

  • Staying with the boat
  • Righting the dinghy
  • Reboarding
  • Checking the boat and rig
  • Getting underway again

Learning Approach

The in-person program focuses on practical, hands-on learning on the water.

Practice. Correct. Develop Skills. Do It Again.

Program for Dinghy Sailing 201

Develop intermediate dinghy sailing skills through practical, hands-on sailing instruction.

Dinghy Sailing 201 is designed for sailors who have completed basic dinghy sailing instruction and are ready to develop greater control, confidence, and performance.

The program combines online instruction with in-person sailing practice focused on applying intermediate sailing techniques on the water.

1. Intermediate Sailing

  • Review of fundamental sailing skills
  • Developing greater boat awareness
  • Understanding the relationship between wind, boat, and sailor
  • Preparing for performance-oriented sailing

2. Boat Balance

  • Recognizing proper boat balance
  • Controlling heel
  • Maintaining fore-and-aft trim
  • Using sail controls and body position together
  • Developing a balanced and responsive boat

3. Boom Angle

  • Understanding boom position
  • Using the mainsheet effectively
  • Finding the correct sail angle for the point of sail
  • Recognizing over-trimmed and under-trimmed sails
  • Making small mainsheet adjustments

4. Outhaul

  • Adjusting the outhaul while sailing
  • Changing the shape of the lower sail
  • Using sail shape to control power
  • Adapting outhaul tension to wind conditions

5. Vang

  • Understanding vang tension
  • Controlling boom height
  • Managing leech tension
  • Controlling sail twist
  • Adjusting the vang for different points of sail

6. Downhaul

  • Adjusting luff tension
  • Changing forward sail shape
  • Using the downhaul as wind strength changes
  • Recognizing when additional downhaul tension is useful
  • Coordinating the downhaul with other sail controls

7. Sailor Position

  • Using body position to control heel
  • Moving fore and aft to maintain trim
  • Hiking techniques
  • Moving smoothly with the boat
  • Using body position to improve boat performance

8. Daggerboard

  • Understanding lateral resistance
  • Reducing leeway
  • Using the daggerboard upwind
  • Adjusting daggerboard position while reaching
  • Reducing unnecessary underwater drag

9. Wind Conditions

  • Recognizing changes in wind strength
  • Recognizing gusts and lulls
  • Recognizing changes in wind direction
  • Reading the water and wind patterns
  • Adjusting sail trim and sailor position
  • Anticipating changing conditions

10. Performance

  • Coordinating boat balance and sail trim
  • Maintaining an efficient sailing course
  • Reducing unnecessary steering
  • Optimizing boat speed
  • Developing feel for the boat
  • Making small, purposeful adjustments
  • Applying intermediate sailing techniques together

11. Skills Assessment

  • Demonstrating boat balance
  • Demonstrating effective sail trim
  • Adjusting sail controls while sailing
  • Demonstrating appropriate sailor position
  • Using the daggerboard appropriately
  • Responding to changing wind conditions
  • Demonstrating controlled and efficient sailing

Learning Approach

The in-person program focuses on practical, hands-on development of intermediate sailing skills on the water.

Students are encouraged to observe the boat, make adjustments, feel the result, and repeat the process.

Practice. Correct. Develop Skills. Do It Again.
